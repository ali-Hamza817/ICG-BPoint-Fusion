## Caveats

- Abel et al. (2026) report MAE 8.13 ms with PEPbench's 12 algorithms on 11,239 cycles; this work uses 11,903 cycles and 10 own re-implemented rules, so the numbers are **not directly comparable** until Abel's design is re-run on the identical cohort and folds.
- Sheikh's AdaBoost was tuned on a 4-configuration subset of his grid and his feature definitions were re-implemented from the paper (assumptions marked in `pipeline/03_sheikh_features.py`).
- Cross-dataset rows are the best of many configurations ranked on the test dataset, i.e. optimistic; a train-only selection is still to be done.
