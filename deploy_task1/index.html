ICG Patient Beats

Every heartbeat of every patient in the public Abel et al. 2026 datasets (EmpkinS n=15, Guardian n=24): its R-peak, its B-point and Q-onset from the manual annotation, and its PEP.

PEP = B-point time minus Q-wave onset, both from the manual annotation by rater 1 (rater 2 shown for comparison). RB = B-point time after the R-peak. R-peak is found in the cleaned ECG next to the annotated Q onset. Waveform = ICG dZ/dt, band-pass 0.5 to 40 Hz, aligned at the R-peak (0 ms). Usable beats have a B label and a PEP between 20 and 250 ms. The rest are flagged as annotation artefacts and drawn struck-through in the tables.
